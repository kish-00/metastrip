How it works: MetaStrip reads the EXIF metadata embedded in your photo (GPS location, camera model, date/time, device serial numbers, etc.) and shows you what's there. When you click "Strip metadata," the image is re-encoded in your browser through a canvas — this creates a clean copy with all EXIF stripped. Nothing is uploaded to a server; everything happens locally.

Limitations: Some social platforms and messaging apps strip metadata automatically; others don't. This tool helps you take control before you share. Screenshots may still contain visible identifying details. Always review the final image before posting.
